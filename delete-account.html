Delete your account

You can request deletion of your 10-9 account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

Email craig@10-9app.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account." Include the email address your account is registered under if it's different from the one you're writing from.

Once we receive your request, we'll delete your account and the personal data associated with it, typically within 30 days. This includes:

  • Your account details and login information
  • Your round, bout, and cycle history
  • Your corner connections and any cue customisations
  • Your "My Why" entry and any journal notes

Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal and regulatory reasons. Deleting your account is permanent and can't be undone.

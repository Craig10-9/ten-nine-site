Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

What we collect

Your name, email, self-ratings, cue choices, and bout history, and — if you choose to use them — a My Why photo/text and Corner membership.

How it's stored

Securely in your own account, not only on this device. Your data lives on our servers, protected so that only you can access it. It is never sold or shared with third parties.

Corners

If you join a Corner, your display name, bout progress, and fight record become visible to the other members of that Corner (max 5 people). Your My Why (photo, name, reason) is never shared, even inside a Corner.

Your rights

You can download a full copy of everything tied to your account at any time via Settings → Export my data, and permanently delete your account and everything in it via Settings → Delete account. Logging out simply ends your session — your data stays safely intact for next time.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the support channel provided wherever you downloaded 10-9, or email craig@10-9app.com.

This policy applies to the 10-9 mobile application. It is written in plain language on purpose — if anything here is unclear, get in touch using the contact details above.
